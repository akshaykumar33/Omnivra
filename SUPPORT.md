## 3. Issue Triage SLAs

- **Security Reports**: Acknowledged within 48 hours.
- **Critical Bugs (Crash/Data Loss)**: Initial triage within 72 hours.
- **Feature Requests & General PRs**: Triaged on a weekly basis.
